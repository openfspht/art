## 1. The mark

A circle cut by three gaps: separate arcs that still close one ring, as parties do in a
standard. It doubles as the **o** of *openfsp*. The accent arc is the implementer.

## 2. Construction

On a 100 × 100 field:

| | |
|---|---|
| Ring radius (centreline) | 32 |
| Stroke weight | 14 |
| Gap width | 14 |
| Gap positions | 45°, 165°, 285° |
| Ink box | 78 × 78 |

Two rules must survive any redraw:

- **Gaps have a constant width.** Arcs are cut by parallel lines, not radii, so a gap
  measures 14 at every radius.
- **No gap touches the circle's extremes.** This keeps the ink box an exact square. It is
  why the gaps sit 15° off the axes and the accent arc is centred at 345°.

## 3. Assets

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| Logo | Default everywhere. |
| Symbol | App icons, stickers, or where the name is already written. |
| Wordmark | Where the symbol would compete or cannot be reproduced. |
| Favicon | Browser tabs. |
| Avatar | Profile images (GitHub, social). Survives a circular crop with full clear space. |

| Variant | Use |
|---|---|
| default | Light backgrounds. |
| `-mono` | Single ink, no accent. |
| `-inverse` | Dark backgrounds. |
| `-black` / `-white` | Pure black or white reproduction. |

## 4. Space and size

- **Clear space:** 14% of the symbol's height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** logo 90 px wide, wordmark 70 px wide, symbol 16 px (24 px preferred).

## 5. Colour

| | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B3B36` | Mark, lettering, text. |
| Accent | `#E8A33D` | One arc only. |
| Accent deep | `#C07E1B` | Accent that must carry contrast on a light ground. |
| Chalk | `#F5F2EA` | Light ground, inverted mark. |

The accent fails WCAG contrast on light grounds (1.93 on chalk, 2.16 on white). The mark
still reads because its form is carried by the ink arcs, so nothing may rely on the accent
alone. Accent deep passes 3:1 on both chalk and ink. Ink on chalk is 11.09.

The Haitian flag colours are deliberately absent: OpenFSP is not a government project.

## 6. Typography

[Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk) (SIL OFL 1.1).

- Wordmark: Bold, lowercase, -2.5% tracking, converted to outlines.
- Headings: Medium or Bold.
- Body text: any neutral face.

## 7. Misuse

Do not retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to the mark; do not change its
gaps; do not colour more than one arc; do not place the colour version on a mid-tone
background; do not use it to suggest endorsement.

## 8. Files

SVG sources are in [`logo/`](logo/), named `openfsp-<asset>[-<variant>].svg`. The wordmark
has no `-mono`, and the avatar comes only as default and `-inverse`. PNG exports in
[`png/`](png/) are generated by `scripts/export-png.sh` and never edited by hand.

SVG files are self-contained, use no transforms and at most three decimals. The logo and
the wordmark have a `viewBox` that fits their ink exactly; the symbol keeps its 100 × 100
field, and the favicon and the avatar are padded on purpose, the avatar so that it
survives a circular crop. After any edit, check that the symbol's ink box is still 78 × 78
and every gap still measures 14.
